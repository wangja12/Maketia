## What would flip a no

- Seeing a creator I know in Abidjan or Dakar actually using it and getting paid, plus knowing the real company and people behind Maketia and SasPay. Then I'd try the free plan first.
- La confirmation automatique du paiement avec envoi du PDF sans moi, surtout la nuit, et la preuve que mes ventes augmentent avec la page.
- Automatic confirmation, so the buyer gets the link without me, plus real creators from Abidjan or Dakar I can check, and a known company behind it.
- Que la confirmation soit automatique et que je voie mes ventes monter grâce à la page, au point d'avoir plusieurs produits à vendre.
- La confirmation automatique du paiement avec envoi du lien de téléchargement sans que je touche à mon téléphone, et qui marche avec les moyens de paiement que mes clients utilisent déjà.
- Making real sales on the free page first, like 4 or 5 sales a month, so the 4 900 FCFA is paid by my sales and not by my pocket. Or a lower price, around 2 000 FCFA.
- Automatic confirmation: the SasPay payment comes in and the buyer gets the file without me touching anything, even at 2 a.m.
- Automatic confirmation of SasPay payments, plus creators I know in Abidjan or Dakar showing their real sales on it.
- Automatic payment confirmation with SasPay, plus a well-known creator in my niche showing their real sales on Maketia.
- Making my first few sales with the free page, or a creator I follow on TikTok showing they earn with it.
- If it worked with Mobile Money (Orange Money, Wave, MTN) that I already have, with automatic confirmation, I would try the free plan.
- If it worked with the mobile money I already use and sent the file automatically when the payment arrives, and I saw a creator I follow on TikTok using it.
